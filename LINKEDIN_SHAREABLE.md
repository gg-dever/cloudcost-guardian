## 🎨 How to Use These

### For LinkedIn Carousel Post:

1. **Copy one of the ASCII art blocks above**
2. **Use a monospace font** (Courier New, Consolas, Monaco)
3. **Tools to create images:**
   - **Carbon** (https://carbon.now.sh/) - Beautiful code screenshots
   - **Excalidraw** (https://excalidraw.com/) - Hand-drawn diagrams
   - **Canva** (https://canva.com/) - Professional designs
   - **Google Slides** - Simple and effective

4. **Image specs:**
   - Size: 1080x1080px (square) or 1200x628px (landscape)
   - Format: PNG or JPG
   - Background: White or light gray for readability

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Keep it simple** - Don't overload with text
2. **Use emojis** - They increase engagement
3. **Include numbers** - Specific metrics are compelling
4. **Add CTA** - "Check out the code on GitHub!"
5. **Test on mobile** - Most LinkedIn views are mobile
6. **Post natively** - Don't cross-post from other platforms
