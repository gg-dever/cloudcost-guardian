## Posting Strategy

### Best Times to Post
- **Tuesday-Thursday**: 9-10 AM or 12-1 PM (your timezone)
- **Peak engagement**: Tuesday 10 AM

### Hashtag Strategy
**Use 3-5 relevant hashtags** (more looks spammy):
- Primary: #AWS #CloudEngineering #Serverless
- Secondary: #Python #Portfolio
- Niche: #FinOps #CostOptimization

### Engagement Tactics
1. **Ask a question** at the end to encourage comments
2. **Reply to every comment** within first 2 hours (boosts algorithm)
3. **Tag relevant people** (AWS advocates, tech leaders you follow)
4. **Share in Stories** after posting
5. **Post natively** (don't cross-post from other platforms)

### Follow-Up Posts (Space 1-2 weeks apart)
1. **Technical deep-dive**: "How I optimized Lambda cold starts"
2. **Lessons learned**: "5 things I learned building serverless apps"
3. **Architecture decision**: "Why I chose DynamoDB over RDS"
4. **Code snippet**: Share the Router Pattern implementation
