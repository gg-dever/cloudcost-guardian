## 💡 Pro Tips

### **1. Storytelling > Technical Details**
People remember stories. Use the "challenges overcome" section from PORTFOLIO.md in interviews.

### **2. Quantify Everything**
Always include numbers: "$2.75/month", "2,800 lines", "93% reduction". Numbers = credibility.

### **3. Connect to Business Value**
Every technical decision should tie back to cost/time/quality impact.

### **4. Show, Don't Tell**
"I'm good at AWS" → "I integrated 9 AWS services in this cost optimization platform"

### **5. Be Authentic**
Don't memorize answers. Understand concepts, speak naturally about what you built.
